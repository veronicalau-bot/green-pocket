🌍 聯合國永續發展目標 (UN SDGs)

🌍 UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

聯合國永續發展目標 (SDGs) 是全球為實現永續未來而設定的17個目標。它們呼籲所有國家共同行動，以促進繁榮、保護地球，並確保所有人都能享有和平與繁榮。 （了解更多）

The United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are a collection of 17 global goals set to achieve a sustainable future for all. They call for action by all countries to promote prosperity while protecting the planet and ensuring peace and prosperity for all people. (Learn more)

🌱 永續性的四大支柱

🌱 Four Pillars of Sustainability

這四大支柱的概念源自於 Stockholm Resilience Centre 提出的「SDGs Wedding Cake 模型」，將永續發展目標重新分類為：環境為基礎，社會為中間層，經濟位於最上層，文化貫穿其中。 （了解更多）

These four pillars are based on the "SDGs Wedding Cake Model" by the Stockholm Resilience Centre, categorizing sustainability as: environment as foundation, society as middle layer, economy on top, and culture throughout. (Learn more)

🌍 環境永續

🌍 Environmental Sustainability

減碳、節能、使用再生材料、推動零廢棄製作

Reduce carbon, save energy, use recycled materials, and aim for zero-waste productions

🤝 社會永續

🤝 Social Sustainability

社區參與、無障礙演出、包容性節目設計

Community engagement, accessibility, and inclusive programming

💰 經濟永續

💰 Economic Sustainability

穩健經營、多元收入來源、教育與數位拓展

Stable operations, diverse income, education and digital innovation

🎭 文化永續

🎭 Cultural Sustainability

保存傳統、創新演繹、傳承文化資產

Preserve tradition, encourage innovation, pass on cultural assets
